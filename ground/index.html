Why the connections matter

An alarm tells you what.
The wiring helps explain why.

A silent sensor is a symptom. Its power supply, data bus, and neighbors help tell the rest of the story.

  1. 01

    Follow what the sensors share.

    A motion sensor and a sun sensor can use different data buses and still depend on the same regulator.

  2. 02

    Listen to the healthy sensors, too.

    If other devices on a bus still work, blaming the whole bus explains less than blaming their shared supply.

  3. 03

    Keep the evidence with the answer.

    Each diagnosis links to the readings, alternative explanations, and recovery guidance behind it. You can inspect them yourself.
